Mobile Application Pentesting 
means identifying gaps and flaws in any android, IOS or windows phone application 

1) SSL Pinning 
2) Code Obfuscation (making the code harder to understand)
3) Business Logic Flow 
4) Insecure data storage, etc....

Today will be all about SSL Pinning in mobile application penetration testing 

SSL offloading is basically removing the official SSL certificate of the company's webserver 
SSL onloading is basically adding a forged/non-genuine certificate that the intruder replaces in your request that you send to the webserver 

SSL pinning when applied makes it impossible that the intruder can offload the and onload the SSL Certificate 
in your sent request with an SSL certificate of their choice, can ensure that your requests that you send wpn't get 
intercepted by your burpsuite's proxy

When SSL pinning is used, before the server allows login for the browser to only login after it has verified the SSL 
certificate of the entity that has sent the request.
