The art if human exploitation 

Social Engineering manipulates human beings into performing actions or revealing confidential information. This could be technical or non-technical 
information 

Types of Social Engineering 

Phishing 
fake bogus wesites tricking people into performing particular actions or revealing particular informations

Spear Phishing
phishing targetting/conducted on a spcecific user base 

Vishing 
phishing conducted via calls 

Mishing 
phishing conducted via text messages

Baiting 

Tail Gating
Someone enters through a gate using their credentials and someone just enters through that same open door before it closes on the door you opened

Common Threats 
CEO Scam 
Fake CEO contacting for performing actions or revealing confidential information
Shoulder Surfing 
Dumpster diving (KEVIN Mitnick)
A shredder is used to discard confidential information/documents to prevent eavesdroppers from dumpster diving
Whaling 
Targetting high profile executives in organizatons for performing specific actions or revealing confidential information
Firewalling/Pretexting 
Pretexting is a special form of social engineering attack where an attacker creates a fabricated identity or scenario to manipulate a victim into 
divulging confidential information, granting access to restricted systems, or performing actions they would not normally undertake

Wesites/Tools for scanning suspicious bogus webpages 

urlscan.io (Gives us even a screnshot of the URL we want to visit)
brightcloud (Gives us a metric on which it evaluates the threats associated with visiting the potentially suspicious website)
virustotal.com (checks the provided link/suspicious link, by passing it through a list of many integrated antivirus softwares in this website)
which basically runs the website through all the integrated antivirus softwares and flags the website if it contains any malware/virus 
which might affect or get injected into your system once you visit the domain, 
If even anyone of the antivirus utility software flags the URL as fake/bogus then probably it would be a better choice not not visit the webpage, just
to be safe as a precautionary measure
