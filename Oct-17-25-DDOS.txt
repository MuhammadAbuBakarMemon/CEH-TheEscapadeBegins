Command explanation 

hping3 --help
gives us a list of the possible commands and what these commands mean and the operations 
performed by these commands for this particular tool (Hping)

hping3 -1 -flood -a 192.168.1.103 192.168.1.255
-1 means ping the IP (ICMP mode)
-flood means flood
-a means use a spoofed IP, the given next IP is the soofed IP address and 
the next given IP after this IP and a spcae is the IP to which the attacks are to be sent
